STYLE A3 — PAPER + INK HEAPS (宣纸 / 墨堆):
Warm near-white paper #F4EFE4. The SCREEN is black-and-white: paper, ink HUD, graphite ink-people, charcoal cannons.
Saturated color exists ONLY on a placed modifier heap and on bullets that have already passed it.

ONE DRAWING LANGUAGE for people, cannons, and heaps:
Thick near-black outline. 2–3 hard gray (or hue) steps for volume. Rounded ink-toy forms. Tiny ink droplets at the feet.
Same as the locked enemy/cannon sheet. NOT a second style.

FORBIDDEN — workshop tools that break the cast:
blowtorch, pliers, wrench, drill, antenna dish, detonator box, metronome, gear assembly, mallet product shot, any hardware-store object.

MODIFIER on the GRID = a flat playing card with ONE large black Chinese character.
Cream card, graphite double-line border. Like 赵云与阿斗 tiles: solid ink, CLEAN closed edges.
NO splatters, flying-white, grain, or messy brush spray (dirty at 88px, hard to cut).
The character is written with brush momentum: slant, thick-thin, a slash like 赵云与阿斗 tiles.
The effect IS a stroke of the same character, not a sticker under or beside it.
Clean closed edges, no splatters. No animation.

Cards (one fused graphic):
- Fire 火: the two dots ARE licking flames grown from 人
- Ice 冰: 冫 IS two crystals grown from the glyph
- Split 分: 八 IS two slashing blades
- Track 瞄: 目 IS the crosshair
- Pierce 穿: the vertical IS a spear stabbing down
- Explode 炸: burst rays explode OUT of the 火 radical
- Accel 速: keep every stroke of 速; two green chevrons grow only from the 辶 tip
- Heavy 重: keep every stroke of 重; only the inner 田 is crushed/filled; nothing under the glyph

CANNON + ENEMY: lock the approved unit sheet. Do not redesign them. Cannons are not cards.

PAGES must stay black-and-white. No orange wash on the whole field. No extra card row glued under the battle. No mountains.
