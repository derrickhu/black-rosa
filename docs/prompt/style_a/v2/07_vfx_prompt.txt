SHARED PRINCIPLES (Ink Line / 墨弹防线 — do not deviate):
Vertical portrait tower-defense shooter, camera facing the field straight-on (NOT 3/4 tactics top-down, NOT isometric).
Silhouette first: one exaggerated memory shape per object, drop zoom-only details.
Value before hue: paper ground stays bright; ink shapes read in black-and-white first.
COLOR IS GAMEPLAY, NOT DECORATION: the world, empty cells, cannons, HUD chrome stay near black-and-white. First-phase enemies are ink people in graphite/gray (readable bodies, not rainbow). Saturated color appears ONLY on modifier tools, modified bullets, burn/ice/explode, phase-2 clothing tints, and reveal.
Thick near-black outlines (pure black or very dark warm brown, never mid-tone or colorful outlines). Volume = 2–3 hard flat color steps, not soft 3D, not paper-cut. No painterly strokes, no photo texture, no noise.
No grassland, no chibi RPG party, no cold blue-gray chrome copied from other games.
Readable at phone size: enemies ~50-90px, card marks ~88px, cannons ~70px.
NO watermarks, no English UI chrome, no store badges, no QR codes.

STYLE A2 — PAPER STAGE + INK MACHINES (宣纸战场 / 机关墨件):
Warm paper field #F4EFE4. World stays paper. Color is still gameplay: only mods, modified shots, VFX, and late-stage clothing tints.

VOLUME RULE (critical):
Volume comes from 2–3 hard color steps (light plane / mid body / dark side), plus a thick near-black outline.
NOT paper-cut flat silhouettes.
NOT plastic 3D, NOT soft airbrush, NOT realistic shading, NOT photo texture.
Think chunky vector toys: you can tell top from side, but every plane is a flat fill.

CANNON:
Top-down with a slight tilt so the barrel top is visible. Dark charcoal cylinder barrel, lighter gray top plane, thick black rounded-square base. A train of identical guns, linked. Weight like a desk tool, not a sticker.

TOOLS / CARDS (not elemental emoji):
Each modifier is a small MACHINE or HAND-TOOL, one silhouette, one accent color.
Forbidden on cards: flame tongues, ice crystals, gem diamonds, magic wisps, raw geometric stamps (bare triangle/diamond/circle).
Meaning must read at a glance from the tool's job.

ENEMIES (PVZ-like ink people, not glyphs):
Readable body: head, torso, arms, legs. Plants vs Zombies cartoon structure, but ink-print.
Early: near-black / graphite fills, thick outline, gray cloth, white eye dots. NOT a solid black blob, NOT a white ghost, NOT a triangle.
Late/elite/boss: same bodies, small clothing color (hat band, coat patch), never full rainbow.
Each type has one body gag (shield lid, lean runner, cluster of small guys, wide sidestep, huge boss).

VFX:
Same rendering as the approved Cursor A VFX sheet: white-hot core → saturated mid → darker rim, sharp hard edges, on black.
Shapes of travel shots must be TOOL-SLUGS / machine bits (bolts, nozzles, nails, canisters), NOT fireballs or ice crystals.
