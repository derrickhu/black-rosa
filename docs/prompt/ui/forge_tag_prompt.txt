One small white porcelain name tag on a solid highlighter-magenta field the colour of #FF00FF. Wide magenta margin all around. The tag is a short landscape pill with rounded ends, thick outline the colour of a Chinese New Year red envelope, cream porcelain fill.

The two printed characters 炮台 sit in the centre, the colour of that same red envelope, bold sticker lettering, same size, same weight.

Printed sticker art. Flat hard-edged cut paper. One object only.
