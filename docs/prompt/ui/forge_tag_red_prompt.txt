One small landscape porcelain plaque on a solid highlighter-magenta field the colour of #FF00FF. Wide magenta margin all around. One object only.

The plaque is a short rounded rectangle, filled with Chinese New Year red-envelope red, slightly darker red-envelope outline. The two printed characters 炮台 sit in the centre in dark soy-sauce ink, bold sticker lettering, same size.

Printed sticker art. Flat hard-edged cut paper.
