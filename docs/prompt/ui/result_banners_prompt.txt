The attached images show the art style of this mobile game: flat printed sticker art, solid colour fills, one thick dark walnut-brown outline, hard crisp edges like cut paper, a single flat darker shade for folds.

Draw two wide ribbon banners stacked in two rows on a pure solid magenta background (#FF00FF), each banner centred in its half with a wide band of empty magenta all around.

Top row: a victory banner. A long ribbon the red of a Chinese New Year envelope, with a narrow trim along the top and bottom edges the yellow of a new gold coin, two swallow-tail ends folding behind, and a small gold-coin-yellow round rivet at each fold. The large centre face is one clean flat red area, empty, ready for a title.

Bottom row: a defeat banner. The same ribbon shape made of faded ink-grey paper the colour of wet slate, torn with a ragged jagged tear through the right end, one swallow-tail hanging lower than the other, two small patches the brown of old tea stains. The large centre face is one clean flat slate-grey area, empty, ready for a title.

Wide flat shapes, the centre faces take up most of each banner.
