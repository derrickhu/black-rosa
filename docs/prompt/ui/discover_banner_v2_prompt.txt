A single wide ceremonial title ribbon, centered, hand-drawn flat sticker for a Chinese ink-defense mobile game. Pure solid magenta background (#FF00FF) fills every side with a wide empty margin.

The ribbon is cut from paper. One thick ink outline the colour of dark cocoa wraps every edge, slightly lively like a brush, still clean and even. The centre is a long horizontal plaque the colour of warm xuan paper, a soft wash from rice-paper cream to a hint of warm milk, flat, one unbroken field. A thin inner trim the yellow of a new gold coin runs along the top and bottom of that plaque. The middle of the top edge rises into one small bookmark tab of the same warm paper, same cocoa outline, same gold-coin trim.

Both ends become short swallow-tail ribbons the red of a Chinese New Year envelope. Each tail folds once behind the plaque, the folded part a slightly deeper envelope red, and carries one small round rivet the yellow of a gold coin where the tail meets the plaque. Two tiny four-point stars, also gold-coin yellow, sit in the upper corners of the paper plaque, small enough to leave the middle of the plaque as open paper.

Solid cut-paper fills, hard crisp edges, one flat darker red only on the folded tails. The plaque stays the largest area and sits in the optical centre of the ribbon.
