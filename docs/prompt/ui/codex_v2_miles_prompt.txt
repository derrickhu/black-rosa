The attached images only show the drawing treatment of this mobile game: flat printed sticker art, solid colour fills, one thick even outline the colour of soy sauce, hard crisp edges like cut paper. The small bottle in the second image is the ink reward icon: a short purple glass ink bottle with a black ink window and a walnut cork. When a bottle appears below, draw that same bottle.

Draw six objects in a 3 by 2 grid on a pure solid magenta background the colour of a highlighter pen (#FF00FF). Each object sits in the centre of its own cell with a wide band of empty magenta on all four sides. Straight-on, flat, the same visual weight across the top row.

Top left: a horizontal reward ticket, about two and a half times wider than it is tall, big rounded corners, thick soy-sauce outline. The body is the flat grey-brown of dry clay. On the left sits one small purple ink bottle. To the right of the bottle is a large empty panel the colour of steamed-bun cream, plain and blank, taking up most of the ticket.

Top middle: the same ticket shape and the same small purple ink bottle on the left, the same large empty steamed-bun-cream panel on the right. The outer body is the solid red of a Chinese New Year red envelope, with a thin gold-coin-yellow line just inside the outline, and four short gold-coin sparkle dashes near the four corners, sitting on the red body and clear of the cream panel.

Top right: the same red ticket, the same ink bottle, the same empty cream panel. A round red-envelope seal stamp sits on the right end, overlapping the red body, with a cream spiral in the stamp and slightly rough stamp edges. The cream panel stays open and blank.

Bottom left: one large round Chinese seal stamp on its own, the solid red of a red envelope, a thick cream inner ring, one cream spiral in the centre, slightly rough stamp edges, thick soy-sauce outline.

Bottom middle: one ink splash, a big round blob of wet black ink, three smaller round ink droplets beside it, thick soy-sauce outline, flat sticker.

Bottom right: one cluster of four sparkles, each sparkle a four-point star the bright yellow of a new gold coin, thick soy-sauce outline, sitting together as one sticker.
