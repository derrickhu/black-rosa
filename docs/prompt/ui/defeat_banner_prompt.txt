A single wide defeat title ribbon, centered, hand-drawn flat sticker for a Chinese ink-defense mobile game. Pure solid magenta background (#FF00FF) fills every side with a wide empty margin.

The ribbon is cut from paper. One thick ink outline the colour of dark cocoa wraps every edge, slightly lively like a brush, still clean and even. The centre is a long horizontal plaque the colour of a river stone washed with dilute pine-soot ink: a warm grey, flat, one unbroken field. A thin inner trim the colour of an old brass coin runs along the top and bottom of that plaque. The middle of the top edge rises into one small bookmark tab of the same stone-grey paper, same cocoa outline, same brass trim.

Both ends become short swallow-tail ribbons the colour of dried pine-soot ink, a deep warm black-brown. Each tail folds once behind the plaque. The right tail ends in a jagged torn rip, with two small round ink blots the colour of a fresh wet ink drop sitting on that torn tail. Where each tail meets the plaque there is one small round rivet the colour of old brass. Two tiny four-point stars, also old-brass, sit in the upper corners of the grey plaque, small enough to leave the middle of the plaque as open stone-grey paper.

Solid cut-paper fills, hard crisp edges. The plaque stays the largest area and sits in the optical centre of the ribbon.
