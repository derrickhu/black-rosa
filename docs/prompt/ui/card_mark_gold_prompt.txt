A flat 2D game UI prototype, straight-on, on a warm rice-paper background the colour of unbleached xuan paper (#F4EFE4). Four rounded-square tiles in one horizontal row, equal size, even gaps, generous paper margin around the row.

Each tile is a calligraphy card. Fill is fresh xuan paper, almost white with a warm cream cast. Outline is one thick even stroke the colour of pine-soot ink (#1A1410), corner radius about one fifth of the tile side. Flat solid colour, hard edges, sticker-like, the same line weight on every tile.

Inside every tile:
- Center: one big Chinese calligraphy character in pine-soot ink, about half the tile height, centered, with empty paper above and below it.
- Top-right, fully inside the outline: a small round badge about one fifth of the tile width. The badge fill is the yellow of a new brass coin (#F6BE3C), with a thin pine-soot ink outline. One digit sits centered in the badge, in pine-soot ink. A margin of empty paper sits between the badge and the card outline, and between the badge and the big character.
- Bottom band, under the character and above the outline: a progress track of short rounded capsules, in its own empty strip.

Filled capsules are a flat vivid green the colour of a fresh tea leaf (#3CB86A). Empty capsules are a flat pale celadon the colour of a light green-tea glaze (#C8E6C4). The yellow badge and the green capsules stay two different colours.

Tile 1, left: the character 分. Digit 2. One single long capsule, filled solid fresh-tea-leaf green, spanning most of the tile width.
Tile 2: the character 火. Digit 1. One single long capsule, filled solid fresh-tea-leaf green. A small flat flame, cinnabar red with a persimmon-orange core, sits to the right of 火.
Tile 3: the character 炸. Digit 1. Two equal capsules with a gap between them. The left capsule is solid fresh-tea-leaf green. The right capsule is pale celadon.
Tile 4, right: the character 秒. Digit 3. Three equal capsules with gaps. The left capsule and the middle capsule are solid fresh-tea-leaf green. The right capsule is pale celadon.

Uniform flat light, hard colour boundaries, thick consistent outlines. The four tiles share one size and one line weight.
