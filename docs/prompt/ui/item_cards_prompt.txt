The attached images only show the drawing style of this mobile game: flat printed sticker art, solid colour fills with one flat shadow tone, one thick even outline the colour of soy sauce (#3A2A20), hard crisp edges like cut paper, chunky rounded corners.

Draw four blank collectible card frames in a 2 by 2 grid on one pure solid magenta background (#FF00FF). Each card sits in the centre of its own cell with a wide band of empty magenta on all four sides, seen perfectly straight-on, symmetric left to right. Every card is an upright rounded rectangle a little taller than wide (width to height about 3 to 4), big rounded corners, filling about 75% of its cell height.

All four cards share one design: a wide coloured border band all the way round; a thin bright line the yellow of a new gold coin running along the inside of the band; a short rounded header strip across the top inside edge in a darker shade of the band colour; a large plain empty face in the middle the soft colour of steamed-bun cream with a gentle lighter round glow in its upper half; a slightly thicker bottom edge in the darker band shade that reads as card thickness.

Top left card: band the fresh green of a new bamboo leaf, header strip a deeper bamboo green.
Top right card: band the clear blue of a summer swimming pool, header strip a deeper pool blue.
Bottom left card: band the rich purple of a ripe grape, header strip a deeper grape purple.
Bottom right card: band the soft grey of an old slate roof tile, header strip a deeper slate grey, the face a muted grey cream like old paper, gold line replaced by a pale grey line.

The faces are completely empty so the game can place pictures on them. Pure flat colours, crisp outline, clean magenta background between the cards.
