Photoreal 35mm film, dusk, static locked-off camera.
A black iron cannonball shoots from the rooftop cannon at the upper right and flies through the glowing calligraphy character in the upper sky. The instant it passes through, that one ball splits into three identical black iron balls. The three balls fly apart and each slams into a different person in the crowd below. Three people sit down backward at the same time.
The glowing calligraphy character stays fixed in the same place in the sky and keeps its exact shape the whole time.
Whoosh, then three metal bonks.
