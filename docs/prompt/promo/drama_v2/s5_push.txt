Photoreal 35mm film, dusk street, static locked-off camera.
A black iron cannonball leaves the cannon in the foreground and flies up through the two glowing calligraphy characters stacked in the sky, the upper character first, then the lower character. The ball then hits the front row. The whole front row of grey-faced people is pushed backward down the street, tumbling into the people behind them, dust rising.
The two glowing calligraphy characters stay fixed in the sky and keep their exact shapes the whole time.
A rush of wind, then muffled bodies colliding.
