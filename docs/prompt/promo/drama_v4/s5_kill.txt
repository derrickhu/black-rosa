Photoreal zombie-apocalypse short drama, 35mm film look, night, epic scale.
The colossal zombie king roars and swings its huge fist down toward the city wall. The young security guard calmly lights the cannon. A black cannonball flies through the two glowing golden calligraphy characters on the left; it instantly becomes a blazing golden spear of light and pierces the giant's chest with a bright flash.
The giant freezes mid-swing, completely still for a beat, a thin golden crack spreading across its body, then it topples backward and crashes down in a huge cloud of dust.
The two glowing calligraphy characters stay fixed in the same place in the sky and keep their exact shapes the whole time.
Camera slowly tilts up toward the giant.
Sound: monster roar, cannon boom, sharp golden ring, a beat of silence, massive crash.
