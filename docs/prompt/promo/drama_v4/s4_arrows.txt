Photoreal zombie-apocalypse short drama, 35mm film look, night, epic scale. Static locked-off camera.
The cannon fires a black cannonball steeply upward through the two glowing golden calligraphy characters in the sky. The instant it passes through them, the cannonball bursts high in the sky like a golden firework and splits into thousands of glowing golden arrows that rain steeply down over the whole old town like a meteor shower, striking the endless zombie horde in waves of golden sparks and dust, zombies knocked flat row after row.
The two glowing calligraphy characters stay fixed in the same place in the sky and keep their exact shapes the whole time.
Sound: cannon boom, rising whistle, thousands of arrows hissing down, rolling impacts.
