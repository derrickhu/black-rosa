Photoreal Chinese zombie-apocalypse short drama, 35mm film look, night, red lantern light.
The young security guard says calmly in Chinese: "那就换个字。" He sweeps the glowing calligraphy brush in one fast stroke; violet sparks fly and the glowing violet calligraphy character in the sky flares brighter. He lights the cannon fuse; the cannon fires a black cannonball upward straight through the glowing character.
The glowing violet calligraphy character stays fixed in the same place in the sky and keeps its exact shape the whole time.
The businessman behind him gapes. Camera pushes in quickly.
Sound: brush swish, magical shimmer, cannon boom.
