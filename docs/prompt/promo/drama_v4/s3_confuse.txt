Photoreal zombie-apocalypse short drama, 35mm film look, night. Static locked-off camera.
A black cannonball flies from the cannon in the lower right through the glowing violet calligraphy character in the sky. The instant it passes through, the cannonball turns into a swirling glowing violet smoke bomb trailing purple sparks. It bursts in the middle of the zombie horde in a huge billowing cloud of violet smoke.
The zombies' eyes glow violet; they spin around and attack each other: biting shoulders, headlocks, slapping, tumbling over each other into piles, a chaotic comedic brawl.
The glowing calligraphy character stays fixed in the same place in the sky and keeps its exact shape the whole time.
Sound: whoosh, soft magical pop, comic zombie snarls and thuds.
