Photoreal Chinese zombie-apocalypse short drama, 35mm film look, night, red lantern light. Fast, punchy pacing.
The young security guard touches the fuse to the black iron cannon; it fires with a loud boom and a burst of smoke. Quick cut feeling: the black cannonball bonks off the head of a zombie climbing the wall with a hollow clang and bounces away; the zombie just shakes its head and keeps climbing.
The chubby businessman in the grey suit shoves the guard's shoulder and shouts angrily in Chinese: "景区摆设炮，守什么守！"
The guard steadies himself and stays silent, jaw set. Handheld camera, slight push in on the guard's face.
Sound: cannon boom, metallic clang, zombie groans, angry shout.
