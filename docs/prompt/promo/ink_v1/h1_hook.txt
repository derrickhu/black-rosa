Chinese ink-wash animated short (guofeng donghua), hand-painted sumi ink on warm cream rice paper, fluid painterly ink motion, ink bleeding into paper fibres.
The tide of round black ink-blob monsters with white dot eyes surges toward the little city wall, bobbing and splashing ink droplets. The scholar in the moon-white robe on the wall turns and lifts his huge brush. The three calligraphy characters in the sky pulse gently with their coloured glows and stay fixed in place, keeping their exact shapes the whole time.
Slow push in.
Sound: rumbling ink tide, soft guqin pluck, wind.
