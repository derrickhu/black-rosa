Chinese ink-wash animated short (guofeng donghua), hand-painted sumi ink on warm cream rice paper, fluid painterly ink motion, ink bleeding into paper fibres.
The black iron cannon on the wall fires a round black cannonball through the glowing ice calligraphy character in the sky. The instant it passes through, the cannonball turns into a glittering glacier-blue ice shell and shatters over the monster tide; a wave of blue frost ink races across the paper and the ink monsters freeze solid inside ice crystals, eyes wide.
The calligraphy character stays fixed in the same place and keeps its exact shape the whole time.
Sound: cannon thump, crystalline chime, ice crackling and spreading.
