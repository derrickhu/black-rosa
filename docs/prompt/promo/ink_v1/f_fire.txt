Chinese ink-wash animated short (guofeng donghua), hand-painted sumi ink on warm cream rice paper, fluid painterly ink motion, ink bleeding into paper fibres.
The black iron cannon on the wall fires a round black cannonball upward through the glowing fire calligraphy character in the sky. The instant it passes through, the cannonball bursts into a roaring fire dragon painted in cinnabar red ink that dives down and coils through the monster tide; the ink monsters splash apart into red ink and ember sparks.
The calligraphy character stays fixed in the same place and keeps its exact shape the whole time.
Sound: cannon thump, dragon roar, whooshing flames, ink splashes.
