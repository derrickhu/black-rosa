Chinese ink-wash animated short (guofeng donghua), hand-painted sumi ink on warm cream rice paper, fluid painterly ink motion, ink bleeding into paper fibres.
The giant crowned ink-blob king roars and raises its fists over the little city wall. The scholar swings his huge brush toward it; the cannon fires a cannonball that passes through the three stacked glowing calligraphy characters on the left and becomes wrapped in twisting ribbons of red flame, blue frost and violet lightning ink. It strikes the king in the chest and the king bursts into a gigantic joyful splash of coloured ink, his golden crown spinning into the air.
The three calligraphy characters stay fixed in the same place and keep their exact shapes the whole time.
Sound: deep roar, cannon thump, huge ink explosion, sparkling chime.
