Chinese ink-wash animated short (guofeng donghua), hand-painted sumi ink on warm cream rice paper, fluid painterly ink motion, ink bleeding into paper fibres.
The black iron cannon on the wall fires a round black cannonball through the glowing thunder calligraphy character in the sky. The instant it passes through, the cannonball crackles into a ball of violet and gold lightning and explodes over the monster tide; branching lightning painted in violet and gold ink chains from monster to monster, each ink monster jolting stiff and glowing.
The calligraphy character stays fixed in the same place and keeps its exact shape the whole time.
Sound: cannon thump, sharp thunder crack, electric buzzing.
