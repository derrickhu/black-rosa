Photoreal comedy zombie-apocalypse short drama, 35mm film look, dusk light.
The lanky man in the mustard-yellow windbreaker and red sweatband touches the lit fuse stick to the black cast-iron cannon. The cannon fires with a loud boom and a puff of white smoke.
