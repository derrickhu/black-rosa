Photoreal zombie-apocalypse short drama, 35mm film look, dusk light. Static locked-off camera.
A black cannonball shoots out of the cannon on the rooftop at the upper right and flies through the glowing ice calligraphy character floating in the sky. The instant it passes through the character, the cannonball transforms into a sparkling glacier-blue ice crystal shell trailing frost mist.
The ice shell slams into the chest of the huge fat zombie, and he freezes solid into a clear ice statue in a split second with a crackling sound. Frost races across the wet road and freezes the zombies beside him.
The glowing calligraphy character stays fixed in the same place in the sky and keeps its exact shape the whole time.
Sound: whoosh, crystalline chime, ice cracking and spreading.
