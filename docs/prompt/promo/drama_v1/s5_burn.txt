Photoreal zombie-apocalypse short drama, 35mm film look, dusk light. Static locked-off camera.
A black cannonball shoots out of the cannon on the rooftop at the upper right and flies through the glowing fire calligraphy character floating in the sky. The instant it passes through the character, it bursts into a huge roaring fireball.
The fireball slams into the frozen ice-statue zombie in a big fiery explosion. The statue shatters and burns down to a heap of grey ash, ash and embers swirl up into the wind, and the nearby zombies are blown off their feet.
The glowing calligraphy character stays fixed in the same place in the sky and keeps its exact shape the whole time.
Sound: whoosh, roaring fire, explosion, crackling embers.
