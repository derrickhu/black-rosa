A cheerful flat illustrated festival map for a casual phone game, a gentle three-quarter view as if looking down at a small tabletop model town, landscape 4:3, the town filling the canvas edge to edge. Soft even light, rounded shapes, thin dark outlines, gentle two-tone shading. Ground the warm pale colour of almond cookies, lanterns the red of a red envelope, metal the yellow of a new gold coin, fruit the colour of fresh mandarins, wood and roofs the dark brown of roasted chestnuts.

A cream stone path rises from a small market at the lower left, up to a temple in the middle, then on to a grand fair at the upper right.

Lower left, centred about one fifth from the left and three quarters down: two little market stalls, baskets of mandarins, a short red awning, a smooth red cloth banner.

Middle, centred halfway across and halfway down: a temple courtyard with a curved chestnut-brown roof, a pair of big red lanterns with gold tassels, stone steps, a round incense burner.

Upper right, centred about four fifths from the left and one quarter down: a wealthy fair yard with a giant red lantern, stacked gold ingots, a fat red treasure pouch with a gold cord, and a plump white lucky cat with closed smiling eyes, a red bib, and one paw raised, sitting beside the pouch.

Small red firecracker scraps and two kumquat pots in the open ground between the places. Each place is large, about a quarter of the picture wide, with plenty of almond-cookie pavement between them. Pure illustration, shapes and colours only, clean surfaces.
