SHARED PRINCIPLES (Ink Line / 墨弹防线 — do not deviate):
Vertical portrait tower-defense shooter, camera facing the field straight-on (NOT 3/4 tactics top-down, NOT isometric).
Silhouette first: one exaggerated memory shape per object, drop zoom-only details.
Value before hue: paper ground stays bright; ink shapes read in black-and-white first.
COLOR IS GAMEPLAY, NOT DECORATION: the world, empty cells, cannons, first-phase enemies, HUD chrome stay near black-and-white. Color appears ONLY on modifier stamps, modified bullets, burn/ice/explode, phase-2 elite/boss, and reveal.
Thick near-black outlines (pure black or very dark warm brown, never mid-tone or colorful outlines). Flat fills with one highlight and one shadow. No gradients, no painterly strokes, no photo texture, no noise.
No grassland, no chibi RPG party, no cold blue-gray chrome copied from other games.
Readable at phone size: enemies ~50-90px, card marks ~88px, cannons ~70px.
NO watermarks, no English UI chrome, no store badges, no QR codes.

STYLE C — CASUAL SILHOUETTE (萝卜剪影):
Warm paper #F4EFE4. Rounder corners, capsule buttons, friendly mass.
World still paper + ink. Color still arrives only after mods. Do NOT paint the whole cast in pastels.
Cannon = round ink-pot turret with a short snub barrel, cute but still black.
Enemies are readable creature-silhouettes in ink (drop, dart-rat, beetle-shield, pebble swarm, crab-strafer, big blot elite, giant seal boss) — one color each unless phase-2.
Cards are round-corner stamp tiles with a single fat icon.
UI like Archero / Carrot Fantasy: big hit areas, soft radii, still no rainbow chrome.
Forbidden: full-color casual mascots, shiny plastic, candy UI, extra lobby tabs.
