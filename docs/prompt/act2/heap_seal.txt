One single simplified Chinese character "封" centred on a flat light rice-paper background the colour of fresh printer paper with a faint warm tint (#FCFCF6), filling the whole square. Thick hand-brushed ink calligraphy, solid filled strokes with closed clean edges and a gentle brush slant, exactly the brush language of the attached reference card glyph. The reference only provides the brush language. The new character is 封.

The character body is soy-sauce black ink (#1A1410). Structure, every stroke in its standard place:

Left half is 圭, two 土 stacked. The upper 土 is a short top bar, a vertical through its middle, and a longer bottom bar. The lower 土 repeats that shape a little wider, and its bottom bar is the longest stroke on the left.

Right half is 寸. A horizontal bar across the top of the right half. A vertical stroke drops from the middle of that bar and hooks left at the bottom. One short dab sits to the right of that vertical, about halfway down, the size of a normal short brush stroke.

Only that short right-hand dab is recoloured. It stays the same size and the same place as that dab, still reading as the short stroke of 寸, still attached to the character. It is a flat hand-painted two-tone brush dab: body the colour of ripe mulberry (#4A3B78), one lighter band the colour of pale lilac silk (#C4B4F0) along its edge. Every other stroke stays black ink.

The character sits in the centre at about 60% of the canvas with equal empty paper on all sides. One character only, the paper stays empty around it.
