## 共用

Warm rice paper #F4EFE4, faint paper fiber. Single stamp-like object, centered, sitting a bit low, occupying the middle 55-65 percent. Painterly ink-wash like the campfire reference: soft brush edges, 2-3 hard color steps, NO thick comic outline, NO vector sticker, NO glossy 3D, NO mountain-shaped ink blob, NO workshop tools (no drill, pliers, blowtorch). Tiny ink droplets at the feet OK. Empty paper around. NO TEXT, no numbers, no star icons, no watermark.
